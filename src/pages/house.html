01The Clubhouse

Our home
since 1917.

Roderic Barnes, Class of 1903, designed the clubhouse in brick to match the campus around it. Above the front door rises the tower that gives the club its name, carved with the club seal.

Over the years the house has grown with us, adding a sunroom, a billiards room, new kitchens and, most recently, a new dining room that opens onto the back lawn.
